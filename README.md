# LockIn Study Alarm ⏰🔒

A minimalist study alarm and anti-doomscroll shield designed for short attention spans, helping you stay locked into your goals with strict focus blocks and rewarding breaks.

## ✨ Key Features

- **⏱️ 20-Minute Focus Sprints:** Timed study intervals engineered to beat procrastination and maintain high-intensity concentration.
- **🚨 System-Level Distraction Alerts:** Gentle nudges and shields to keep you from wandering off to social media or doomscrolling during focus windows.
- **🎧 Ambient Focus Sounds:** Built-in audio tracks (Lo-Fi, Neo-Soul, and background ambience) designed to enhance deep work and cognitive focus.
- **🎁 Gamified Mystery Loot Box:** A rewarding 3-tap mystery box system that unlocks collectible virtual stickers and study perks upon completing your focus sprint.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend / Interface:** Modern web technologies, interactive components, responsive styling.
- **Platform:** Google AI Studio
